THE UNDERLYING NEED

“Help me change the plan.
Not just make one.”

For an independent tutor, moving a recurring lesson could mean checking time zones, messaging students, and updating calendars by hand.

What a useful finding explains

Who feels the friction
Tutors teaching across time zones
What they want to accomplish
Reschedule a lesson without coordinating it twice
What to investigate
How often this happens and what it costs them
THE COMPETITIVE GAP

A good booking flow.
A difficult change of plans.

An audit would compare the complaints with what scheduling tools actually offer. A missing capability is different from one that’s hard to find.

What a useful comparison checks

What alternatives do well
Creating and booking an individual lesson
Where friction might remain
Changing a recurring series across time zones
What needs verification
Feature availability, usability, and recent changes
THE OPPORTUNITY HYPOTHESIS

Make rescheduling
the simplest part of teaching.

A tutor-focused rescheduling flow could reduce manual coordination. That’s a direction to explore—not proof that a new product should exist.

From finding to next action

A possible product response
Move recurring lessons with student confirmation
A practical first experiment
Observe tutors rescheduling real lessons
The assumption to test
Is the friction significant enough to switch tools?

The principle: traceable evidence, clear interpretation, honest uncertainty. No invented certainty.

A few things to know

Good questions.

Is Bibi available to use today?+

Bibi is in development, with a target launch around September 1. This page introduces the product direction; it does not run live research audits yet.

What will I be able to research?+

The planned starting points are your own product, a competitor, or a market category. The usefulness of an audit will depend on the relevant public evidence available. Thin evidence should be called out, not filled in with guesses.

Is this an ongoing monitoring tool?+

No. Bibi is designed around a one-time research audit: a focused snapshot to support a decision, rather than a stream of alerts or a dashboard you need to keep watching.

Will an audit tell me exactly what to build?+

It should help you identify promising directions and what to validate next. Public feedback is not proof of market demand, and an audit is not a replacement for speaking with customers or testing willingness to pay.
