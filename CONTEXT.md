# Bibi

Bibi helps founders and product managers research products and markets. App discovery currently concerns finding Apple App Store apps, without classifying them as competitors.

## Language

**App discovery**:
Finding Apple App Store apps by an app name or keyword. A discovered app is not necessarily a competitor.
_Avoid_: Competitor discovery, competitor selection

**App Store app**:
An app listed in Apple's App Store and identified by its numeric Apple app ID. It may be the user's own product or any other app they want to inspect.

**Keyword search**:
An explicitly chosen discovery mode that presents apps matching a market phrase or keyword. Its results are search matches, not a complete catalog or verified competitors.

**App-name search**:
An explicitly chosen discovery mode for finding a particular app. The user confirms the intended app from matching names and developers before viewing that app alone.

**Storefront**:
A country's App Store catalog, including the apps available there and their listing ratings. Search results belong to the selected storefront; they are not global results.
_Avoid_: Review language, global App Store

**Written review**:
A customer's written feedback about an App Store app, distinct from a star rating without review text. The listing's rating count is not a count of written reviews.
_Avoid_: Rating, rating count

**Review sample**:
The collection of written reviews retrieved for analysis. It does not imply coverage of every review of the app.
_Avoid_: All reviews, complete review history

**Star-rating filter**:
A choice of star ratings used to select written reviews for fetching, display, or review analysis. It concerns written reviews, not the app listing's total rating count.

**Review analysis**:
An evidence-linked interpretation of a review sample covering praise, recurring complaints, feature requests, and potential product improvements. Its findings describe the sample, not every customer or the whole market.

**Filtered review analysis**:
Review analysis of the subset of a review sample that matches a chosen star-rating filter. It is distinct from analysis of the complete review sample.

**Saved research**:
An App Store app and storefront, together with the review samples and analysis retained for later inspection.

**Competitor suggestion**:
An App Store app identified as a possible alternative because it addresses the same problem or shares a category with the app being researched. A suggestion is not verified competition.
_Avoid_: Verified competitor

**Review comparison**:
An evidence-linked comparison of the review samples of the app being researched and the apps chosen for comparison. Differences in samples are not proof of differences across their entire customer bases.
