# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Founders and product managers researching products and markets. On the app details screen, they inspect an exact Apple App Store app in a specific storefront before exploring written reviews and analysis.

## Product Purpose

Bibi helps users discover apps and inspect listing evidence as a starting point for product research. App identity, storefront, listing ratings, written reviews, and analysis must remain clearly distinguished. See CONTEXT.md for domain terminology.

## Brand Personality

Calm, compact, practical. The user confirmed this direction on 2026-10-23. The interface should support quick inspection and deeper reading without overwhelming the researcher.

## Anti-references

Avoid long, always-expanded listing descriptions, multirow screenshot walls, duplicated identity metadata, and decorative treatments that compete with research content. These boundaries follow the user's requested app details hierarchy; no external reference app has been specified.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with visual evidence: make screenshots easy to browse near the top of app details.
2. Make identity and key facts easy to scan, including app ID and storefront-specific rating information.
3. Reveal detail progressively: show a short description preview with an explicit expansion control.
4. Keep research close: place Analysis and Reviews below the screenshot area rather than below a long listing.
5. Preserve evidence boundaries: never present listing ratings as written reviews or imply unavailable analysis exists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The confirmed direction includes keyboard access and reduced motion. Carousels and disclosure controls need clear labels and visible focus. Screenshots must not auto-advance. Preserve image proportions and provide useful alternative text. Do not rely on color alone for state. No additional accessibility standard or external design reference has been specified.
